About Dan

Thirteen years in, five on his own.

Dan started as an electrical apprentice and has spent thirteen years in the trade — the last five running his own business in Irvine.

The business began as a handyman and electrical outfit, and grew into something more specialised. Getting the C-10 contractor's licence was the formal step; the work had already moved toward the high-end residential lighting and larger home projects that make up most of it today.

It is still owner-operated. The person who quotes your job is the person who does it, and the person whose licence is on it.

C-10 — Electrical
Active · $25,000 bond
Verify at cslb.ca.gov →

California requires a contractor's licence for any electrical job over $500 in labour and materials. Unlicensed electrical work is the kind that fails an inspection, voids a home insurance claim, or quietly sits in a wall until something burns.

  • Active C-10 licence — the classification specific to electrical work, held since 2024.
  • $25,000 contractor's bond with American Contractors Indemnity, on file with the state.
  • Owner-operated — the person who holds the licence is the person doing the work.
  • 13 years in the trade, from apprentice to licensed contractor.
