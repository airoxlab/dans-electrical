**Correction to the brief:** it said the licence "EXPIRES OCT 31, 2026, renewal

false. The licensing section turns it into "owner-operated — the person who
holds the licence is the person doing the work."

## What was deliberately not built

- **No reviews/testimonials section** (see above).
- **No surname.** The brief infers "Daniel Grosfeld" from his email address. That
  is a guess from a Yahoo handle, not a published business name, so the site says
  "Dan" throughout. His email is linked, which is his own published contact.
- **No home address.** 10 Carlyle is residential; the site says "Irvine, CA".
- **No "licensed since 2012" claim.** The 13 years is time *in the trade* — he
  started as an apprentice. The site says "13 years in the trade, from apprentice
  to licensed contractor", and separately that the C-10 is held since 2024. Both
  true, neither conflated.
- **No service area beyond Irvine and Tustin** — that is what his Nextdoor page
  claims, so that is what the site claims.

## Design

Deliberately unlike Rockbottom Electric (the other C-10 sample in this
collection, built dark/industrial):

- Warm premium palette — cream `#FCFAF6`, charcoal `#16140F`, brass `#B07A2E`,
  lamp-glow `#E8B04B`. Chosen to read "high-end residential", which is his niche.
- Fraunces (display serif) + Karla (body) — a softer, more designed voice than
  the condensed/grotesk used on the other trade sites.
- Inline SVG pendant-lamp mark with a bolt filament; pill-shaped buttons.
- The lighting service card is visually promoted with a brass border and a
  "Specialty" tag, since that is his differentiator in Irvine.
- Eight sections: hero, credential strip, services, about Dan, process, work
  gallery, areas + licensing, contact.
- Full dark mode (system preference plus explicit `data-theme`), sticky header,
  `scroll-margin-top` anchors, reduced-motion respected, inline CSS, no build step.

## The two Google listings — worth more than the website

He has **two listings on the same phone number**, and this is costing him:

1. **"Dan's Electrical Contracting"** — contractor category, 10 Carlyle, **no reviews**
   (cid=3233794121576342688)
2. **"Dan's Electrical Contracting"** — **handyman** category, 5.0, open 24 hours
   (cid=9474778859591152055) — his older handyman listing, renamed

Neither links his domain. The problems: his review sits on the *handyman* listing
while his *contractor* listing is the one that should rank for "electrician
Irvine", and duplicates split his search presence.

Recommended, in order: **merge the duplicates into the contractor listing**,
carry the review across, **add the website link**, and ask past customers for
Google reviews with written text. Then point the GoDaddy domain at this site —
he keeps the domain, we host the page.

That package is worth considerably more to him than the page on its own.
